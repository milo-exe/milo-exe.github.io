macOS

LyricPresence
for macOS

A native menu bar app. No dock icon, no clutter — just your current lyric silently updating in the top bar and on Discord.

macOS 13 Ventura or later  ·  Intel & Apple Silicon
Setup
1

Build the app

Clone the repo and generate the Xcode project, then build:

cd ~/LyricPresence-macOS
xcodegen generate
open LyricPresence.xcodeproj

Press Cmd+R in Xcode. Set the signing team to None or your personal team — no paid certificate needed.

2

Get a Spotify Client ID

Go to developer.spotify.com, create a new app, and add the following redirect URI:

lyricpresence://callback

Copy your Client ID.

3

Get your Discord token

Open Discord in a browser, open DevTools (F12), go to the Network tab, send any message, and find a request with an Authorization header. Copy that value.

Never share your Discord token — anyone with it has full access to your account.
4

Configure the app

Click the ♫ icon in your menu bar → gear icon → Settings. Paste your Spotify Client ID and click Connect Spotify (opens browser to authorise). Then paste your Discord token.

5

Start

Click the ♫ icon in the menu bar → Start. The current lyric line will appear in the menu bar, truncated to 40 characters, and your Discord status will update in real time.

Troubleshooting

♫ icon shows but lyric never appears

Make sure you clicked Start in the menu bar popover. Also check that Spotify is actively playing, not paused.

"Connect Spotify" doesn't open or fails

Make sure the redirect URI lyricpresence://callback is added exactly in your Spotify developer dashboard.

Discord status not changing

Double-check your Discord token — tokens expire if you change your password or log out of all devices.

No lyrics for a song

If no synced lyrics are found on LRCLIB, the menu bar shows just ♫ and the Discord status is cleared silently.
